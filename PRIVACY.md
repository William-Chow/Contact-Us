# Privacy Policy for Contact Us

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Contact Us is an Android contact book published on Google Play by Chow Wee Lim ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept and who can see it.

## In short

- The contacts you keep in Contact Us are stored only on your phone. We don't run any servers, and the app has no accounts or sign-in.
- The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect information about your device to show and measure those ads, as described below.
- Your contacts are never sent to us, to Google or to any advertiser.

## Contacts you add

When you add or edit a contact, the app saves the first name, last name, phone number and email address in a database on your phone. This information is not uploaded anywhere.

## Reading your phone's contacts

The app can copy contacts from your phone's address book in two ways. Both only happen when you ask for them.

- **Import phone contacts.** Before Android asks for permission, the app explains what it will do. If you allow the Contacts permission, the app reads the names, phone numbers and email addresses saved on your phone and copies them into Contact Us. If you decline, the rest of the app works as before. You can turn the permission off at any time in Android settings.
- **Fill from phone contacts.** When you create a new contact, you can pick one person from Android's contact picker. The app only sees the person you pick and needs no permission.

The contacts the app reads are stored only on your phone, in the same place as the contacts you add yourself. They are not uploaded, not shared with us or anyone else, and not used for advertising.

## Calls, messages and email

The call, message and email buttons pass the phone number or email address to your phone, messaging or email app. Contact Us does not make calls or send messages itself, and it does not read your call history, text messages or emails.

## Backup files

**Export backup** saves your contacts to a file at a location you choose with Android's file picker, such as your phone's storage or a cloud drive. **Restore from backup** reads a file you choose. We never receive these files. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. If you save it to a cloud drive, that service's own privacy policy applies.

If Android's own backup is turned on, Android may include this app's data in your phone's backup to your Google account, as it does for other apps. That backup is managed by Google, and we cannot access it.

## Advertising

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banner ads and an occasional ad when the app opens. To show, personalize (where allowed) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect or receive:

- your device's advertising ID
- your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it
- information about your device and this app, such as the model, operating system and app version
- how you interact with ads

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing ads. You can change your choice at any time with the **Privacy options** button in the app. Anywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings.

Google handles this data under its own policies. See [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The app also stores, on your phone only, when it last showed an opening ad, so that it doesn't show one too often.

## What we don't do

- We don't collect, store or receive your contacts or any other personal information on our own servers.
- We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services.
- We don't sell or share your information.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your contacts stay on your phone until you delete them in the app or uninstall it. Uninstalling removes everything the app has stored on your phone, but not backup files you exported. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data collected by Google for ads is kept and deleted under Google's policies.

## Security

The app's data is kept in its private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Backup files you export are not encrypted.

## Children

Contact Us is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
